# 3. Weighted Accuracy Calculation

The scores used for the evaluation are:

* 10 channels at 100%
* 1 channel at 90%
* 1 channel at 80%
* 1 channel at 70%

First calculate the fully accurate reports:

10 × 100 = 1,000

Then add the partially accurate reports:

1 × 90 = 90

1 × 80 = 80

1 × 70 = 70

Total accuracy points:

1,000 + 90 + 80 + 70 = 1,240

There are 13 tested channels.

Therefore:

1,240 ÷ 13 = 95.384615...

Rounded to the nearest whole percentage:

**95%**

This is more representative than simply counting any detected mistake as a failed report.

---

# 5. Important Methodological Note

This is not a laboratory-grade factual accuracy measurement because the evaluation does not independently verify every individual claim against a complete ground-truth dataset.

Instead, it measures the **quality and correctness of the generated channel profiles according to the manual evaluation performed on the 13 tested channels**.

Therefore the most accurate description is:

**"SupaFAN v2.0 achieved a 95.38% weighted accuracy score across 13 manually evaluated YouTube channel analyses."**

That is a defensible statement.
